Privacy Policy for Invoice Maker

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Invoice Maker is developed by zentro Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Invoice Maker mobile application.

Information Stored by the App

Invoice Maker is designed to work primarily offline. Information such as business details, customers, products, and invoices may be stored locally on your device so that you can use the app and generate invoices.

Advertising

Invoice Maker uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or use information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising information, diagnostics, and interaction information for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and related purposes, subject to Google's policies and applicable settings.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

PDF Files

The app can generate PDF invoices. These files are created for the user's use and may be saved or shared using features available on the device.

Data Sharing

Invoice Maker does not sell users' invoice or business information. Information handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob is subject to those services' own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Invoice Maker is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Invoice Maker, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
